How it works

NowCast weights recent hours more heavily when the air is changing quickly. The weight factor is 1 − (max − min) ÷ max, kept between 0.5 and 1. Each reading is multiplied by the weight raised to its hours-ago number, and the total is divided by the sum of the weights.

Missing hours are skipped in both sums, but EPA requires valid readings for at least 2 of the 3 most recent hours. Readings below −10 µg/m³ are bad data and are skipped like missing hours, as AirNow does, and a negative NowCast is treated as 0 for the AQI. If every reading is negative, the weight factor comes out above 1 and is capped at 1. Above 500, the Hazardous range is extended upward (EPA classes 301 and above as Hazardous). The AQI is (AQIhi − AQIlo) ÷ (Chi − Clo) × (C − Clo) + AQIlo, rounded to a whole number.
