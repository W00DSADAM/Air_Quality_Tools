2. NOWCAST CALCULATOR (NOWCAST.HTML)
==============================================================================

What it does
------------

Takes 12 hours of PM2.5 or PM10 readings and works out the EPA NowCast
concentration, then converts it to an AQI. It shows every step of the math and
a table of the hourly weights, so you can check the work.

How to use it
-------------

  - Pick PM2.5 or PM10 at the top.
  - Set the newest hour, or click Use current hour.
  - Type the 12 readings, oldest at the top. Leave a box blank if that hour is
    missing.
  - Or open Paste 12 readings at once and paste a column copied from Excel,
    then click Fill hours.
  - The AQI, category and steps update as you type. Copy result copies a one-
    line summary.
  - The page remembers your last readings in this browser.

Rules it follows
----------------

  - Weight factor = 1 - (max - min) / max, kept between 0.5 and 1.
  - At least 2 of the 3 newest hours must have valid readings, or no result is
    shown.
  - Missing hours are skipped in both sums.
  - Readings below -10 ug/m3 are treated as bad data and skipped, as AirNow
    does. Readings from -10 to 0 are used.
  - Optional checkbox: Count negative readings as 0.
  - The NowCast is truncated (cut, not rounded): PM2.5 to 0.1 ug/m3, PM10 to a
    whole number.
  - A negative NowCast gives AQI 0. Above AQI 500, the Hazardous formula keeps
    going.

Messages
--------

Message                                       Meaning
--------------------------------------------  --------------------------------
Enter readings to get a result                Every box is blank
EPA requires valid readings for at least 2    Fill in the newest hours
of the 3 most recent hours
These hours don't contain a number            A box has a typo
Readings below -10 are bad data...            Those hours were skipped
Missing hours skipped in both sums            Some boxes were blank; the
                                              result still counts
Every reading is negative...                  Weight factor capped at 1 (plain
                                              average)
Above 500...                                  The Hazardous range was extended
                                              upward

3. AQI CALCULATOR (AQI.HTML)
==============================================================================

What it does
------------

Converts concentrations of the four pollutants we monitor into AQI values,
shows each pollutant's AQI with its color, and reports the overall AQI: the
highest of them, and which pollutant set it. It also averages 8 hourly ozone
readings.

How to use it
-------------

  - Type a concentration in any box. You don't need to fill them all. Results
    update as you type.
  - Each box shows its own AQI badge. The Overall AQI card shows the highest,
    its category, the pollutant it came from, and a marker on the color scale.
  - 8-hour ozone: set the last hour (or click Use current hour), type the
    hourly readings, and click Average. The average fills the Ozone (8-hr) box
    automatically.

Inputs and units
----------------

Box               Units     Kept before calculating
----------------  --------  --------------------------------------------------
PM2.5 (24-hr)     ug/m3     1 decimal (35.47 counts as 35.4)
PM10 (24-hr)      ug/m3     Whole number
Ozone (8-hr)      ppb       Whole number
Ozone (1-hr)      ppb       Whole number
SO2 (1-hr)        ppb       Whole number
SO2 (24-hr)       ppb       Whole number (enter an average you already have)

Rules it follows
----------------

  - AQI = (AQI_hi - AQI_lo) / (C_hi - C_lo) x (C - C_lo) + AQI_lo, rounded to
    a whole number, using EPA's 2024 breakpoints.
  - Concentrations are truncated, not rounded, before the lookup.
  - Negative readings count as 0. (An agency choice, not an EPA rule.)
  - The overall AQI is the highest individual AQI.
  - PM2.5 and PM10 can go above AQI 500: the top row's formula keeps going.
  - 8-hour average: needs at least 6 of the 8 hours, divides by the number of
    hours entered, and truncates to a whole ppb.

Messages
--------

Message                         Meaning
------------------------------  ----------------------------------------------
Use 1-hr (on Ozone 8-hr)        Above 200 ppb. EPA uses 1-hour ozone for AQI
                                above 300
Use 8-hr (on Ozone 1-hr)        Below 125 ppb. The 8-hour value decides at
                                this level
Use 24-hr (on SO2 1-hr)         Above 304 ppb. EPA uses the 24-hour SO2
                                average for AQI 200 and up
Use 1-hr (on SO2 24-hr)         Below 305 ppb. The 1-hour value decides at
                                this level
Not a number                    The box has a typo, like 12a
Need at least 6 hours (have N)  Fewer than 6 hourly ozone readings entered
N box(es) aren't numbers...     Fix the hourly ozone typos before averaging

4. UNIT CONVERSION CALCULATOR (UNITS.HTML)
==============================================================================

Type in any box and the other boxes in that card update. Results are rounded
for display to the places below, so very small values can show as 0 (1 W/m2
shows as 0 kW/m2).

Card                    Units                     Shown to
----------------------  ------------------------  ------------------------------
Solar radiation         kW/m2, W/m2               kW: 2 decimals; W: whole
                                                  number
Flow                    cc/min, LPM               LPM: 3 decimals; cc/min: whole
                                                  number
Pressure                mmHg, mbar, psia          mmHg and mbar: 1 decimal;
                                                  psia: 3
Wind speed              m/s, mph, knots, km/h     1 decimal
Temperature             C, F                      1 decimal
Wind direction          degrees to compass point  16 points (N, NNE, NE...)
Actual/standard flow    LPM, SLPM                 2 decimals

  - Wind direction: each compass point covers 22.5 degrees. An exact boundary
    goes to the next point clockwise (11.25 is NNE). Values outside 0-360 show
    Enter 0 to 360.
  - Actual/standard flow uses EPA standard conditions, 25 C and 760 mmHg (40
    CFR 50.3): SLPM = LPM x (P / 760) x (298.15 / (T + 273.15)). Enter
    temperature and pressure first. Changing them later recalculates in the
    direction you used last.
  - Conversion factors: 1 mmHg = 1013.25/760 mbar; 1 psi = 68.94757 mbar; 1
    mph = 0.44704 m/s; 1 knot = 1852/3600 m/s.

5. USE CASES
==============================================================================

Typical tasks, step by step. Edit these to match how your team works.

A. Check the current PM2.5 AQI from a monitor
   1. Open NowCast and pick PM2.5.
   2. Copy the last 12 hourly values from the data logger or spreadsheet,
      oldest first.
   3. Open Paste 12 readings at once, paste, and click Fill hours.
   4. Read the AQI and category. Use the Step by step table to show the math
      if asked.

B. Find the overall AQI for a site
   1. Open AQI. Enter each pollutant's value in its box.
   2. The Overall AQI card shows the highest AQI and which pollutant set it.

C. Get an 8-hour ozone AQI from hourly data
   1. Open AQI. In the 8-hour section, set the last hour.
   2. Enter the 8 hourly ozone values (at least 6) and click Average.
   3. The average fills the Ozone (8-hr) box, and the AQI updates.

D. Check a high SO2 hour
   1. Enter the 1-hour value in SO2 (1-hr). Above 304 ppb it shows Use 24-hr.
   2. Enter the 24-hour average in SO2 (24-hr) to get the AQI.

E. Convert a sampler flow to standard conditions
   1. Open Units, Actual / Standard Flow card.
   2. Enter the ambient temperature (C) and pressure (mmHg), then the actual
      flow in LPM.
   3. Read the standard flow in SLPM. At 25 C and 760 mmHg the two are equal.

F. Convert a barometric pressure reading
   1. Open Units, Pressure card. Type the reading in its unit; the others
      update.

6. HOW THE TOOLS WERE TESTED
==============================================================================

Each test case was run on the actual pages in a web browser: the input was
typed into the box, and the result the page showed was recorded. The expected
answers were worked out separately, with exact decimal math written from the
EPA rules and the unit definitions, not taken from the tools themselves.

  - AQI: every breakpoint edge for all six tables, truncation cases, values
    past each table, past AQI 500, negatives, typos, blanks, the overall AQI,
    and the 8-hour average rules.
  - NowCast: the built-in worked example, steady, rising and falling air,
    missing hours, the 2-of-3 rule, readings below -10, all-negative readings,
    past 500, PM10, and pasting.
  - Units: every card in both directions, known reference values, compass
    boundaries, and actual/standard flow at several temperatures and
    pressures.

Earlier checks against EPA's AirNow: the NowCast averaging matched AirNow on
about 15,000 hours of PM2.5 data, and the PM10 concentration-to-AQI conversion
matched AirNow's AQI calculator.

Tool          Cases     Passed
------------  --------  --------
AQI           78        78
NowCast       13        13
Units         48        48
Total         139       139

Test date: November 25, 2026. To re-test by hand, type any input below into
the live page and compare.

==============================================================================
8. LIMITS
==============================================================================

  - CO and NO2 are not included. They can be added (one table and one row
    each) if needed.
  - The AQI calculator converts concentrations you enter. It does not average
    24 hours of PM or SO2; enter averages you already have.
  - Results are only as good as the data entered. Real-time data is
    preliminary and may change after review, so a result can differ from
    AirNow for the same hour.
  - These tools support analysis and checking. Official reported values come
    from AQS and AirNow.
